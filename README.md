# CreditsPlan

**AI 服务的智能选型与购买决策引擎**  
**An intelligent selection and purchase decision engine for AI services**

**Find the right AI. Pick the right plan. Pay the right price.**

CreditsPlan 帮助用户比较 AI 工具与服务的价格、套餐额度、支持模型和使用条件，并结合需求作出购买决策。中国站与国际站提供产品、套餐及价格信息；智能选型站帮助用户结合预算、常用工具、模型需求和已有订阅选择方案。**当前智能选型重点覆盖 AI 编程。**

CreditsPlan helps you compare AI tools and services by price, plan quotas, supported models and usage conditions, then make purchase decisions around your needs. The China and Global sites provide product, plan and pricing information; the AI Advisor helps you narrow down options based on your budget, tools, model requirements and existing subscriptions. **The advisor currently focuses on AI coding.**

## 可以帮助你做什么 / What you can do

- **比较工具与套餐**：查看价格、计费周期、额度、模型支持、工具兼容性和使用限制。  
  **Compare tools and plans:** review pricing, billing cycles, quotas, model support, tool compatibility and usage limits.
- **结合需求缩小范围**：从预算、使用方式和已有订阅出发，比较适合自己的方案。  
  **Build a shortlist around your needs:** compare options using your budget, workflow and current subscriptions.
- **购买前核实条件**：查看来源链接、核验日期、价格变化和活动规则。  
  **Check the conditions before buying:** review source links, verification dates, price changes and offer terms.

最新价格、收录范围、模型支持与活动期限以官网页面及厂商官方来源为准。此 README 不维护动态价格表或完整产品清单。

Visit the websites and linked vendor sources for current prices, coverage, model support and offer dates. This README does not maintain live pricing tables or a complete product catalog.

在 [creditsplan.ai](https://creditsplan.ai/) 描述需求，或选择预算、常用工具、必须支持的模型和已有套餐；可以边聊边调整条件。当前适用场景包括：

Describe your needs at [creditsplan.ai](https://creditsplan.ai/), or select your budget, tools, required models and existing plans. Refine the criteria as you chat. Current scenarios include:

- **选一个套餐 / Choose a plan** — 按预算和编程工作流比较候选方案。Compare candidates for your budget and coding workflow.
- **比较单／双套餐 / Compare one or two plans** — 了解单订阅与双套餐组合的覆盖和取舍。Explore coverage and trade-offs between one subscription and a two-plan combination.
- **优化已有订阅 / Review existing subscriptions** — 检查需求覆盖与重复，考虑保留或替换。Review coverage and overlap to decide what to keep or replace.

智能选型当前重点覆盖 AI 编程，具体可选工具、模型和套餐以站内为准；购买前请核实厂商最新价格、地区可用性与条款。

The advisor currently focuses on AI coding. Available tools, models and plans are shown on the site; confirm current vendor pricing, regional availability and terms before buying.

## 数据来源与核验 / Sources and verification

CreditsPlan 优先依据厂商定价页、产品文档和订阅页面核对信息，保留来源链接与核验日期，并区分计费周期、币种、标准价及优惠条件。数据是核验时点的快照，不能替代厂商实时结算信息。

CreditsPlan checks information against vendor pricing pages, product documentation and subscription pages, keeping source links and verification dates. Billing cycles, currencies, standard prices and offer conditions are distinguished. Data represents a verified snapshot, not a vendor's live checkout price.
